Know what a finding means

A missing target means no matching file in your selection. A note without incoming links may be intentional. This checks connectivity within your selected files, not the health of Obsidian’s sidebar or index.

Advanced links (paths, aliases, headings, blocks and embeds), Markdown links, attachments and frontmatter aliases are outside scope. Code, frontmatter and comments are skipped. Duplicate titles are flagged for review. Unsupported syntax and partial selections can produce incomplete results. Text matches are case-sensitive suggestions, not automatic links.

This is a pilot of a limited tool, not a promise of complete vault coverage or ongoing custom development.
