What you receive

A one-page executive summary

A readable synthesis of the strongest findings, uncertainties, and evidence gaps, with references back to your notes.

A recurring-problems table

Each problem, the number of distinct interviewees who mention it, and supporting excerpts. Repeated remarks by one person count once per problem.

Payment statements in context

Separate general interest, conditional willingness, quoted budgets, and any documented purchases. Positive feedback alone is not proof of demand.

Open questions and scope implications

What remains unresolved, plus possible scope changes labeled as interpretations and tied to the evidence.

Delivery: a PDF for reading and an editable DOCX, in one delivery. Up to 6,000 words of source notes per report.

Is this a fit?

This is for founders who already have written interview transcripts or notes. It does not include conducting interviews, audio transcription, recruiting participants, or financial advice. Sparse notes will produce a limited report, not invented conclusions.

Remove names, emails, phone numbers, company identifiers, and identifying anecdotes before sharing. Do not send sensitive personal data, credentials, or material you are not authorized to share. Only send material you permit to be processed with AI assistance and human review; the handling process will be confirmed before intake.

The free pilot is a fixed, single-delivery service. There is no automatic conversion to a paid order. Any future paid service will be offered separately after the owner holds the required licence.
